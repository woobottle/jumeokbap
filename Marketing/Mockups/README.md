## Jumeokbap App Store Mockups

Export guidance

- Canvas size: 2880×1800 (16:10)
- Export format: PNG (preferred) or JPEG at 100% quality
- Color profile: sRGB
- Keep text within 10% margins from canvas edges

Files

- 01_menu_bar.svg — Menu bar time at a glance
- 02_floating.svg — Always-on-top floating timer
- 03_drag_snap.svg — Drag to move, snaps to edges
- 04_settings.svg — Simple popover settings
- 05_notification.svg — System notification when time’s up
- 06_minimal_ui.svg — Minimal UI that won’t distract

Tips

- Use a clean desktop wallpaper and hide private info
- Keep captions short (≤ 6 words) and high contrast
- Keep the menu bar readable; avoid busy backgrounds
